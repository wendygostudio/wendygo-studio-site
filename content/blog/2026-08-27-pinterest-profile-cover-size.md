# Pinterest Profile Cover Size: 800×450 and 16:9 Guide

A Pinterest profile cover is the wide image at the top of a business profile. It has a different job from a Pin: the cover introduces the profile, while a Pin is content that people discover, save and click. Keeping those two canvases separate prevents an otherwise good image from being cropped into the wrong shape.

## What Pinterest officially recommends

Pinterest's [profile cover help page](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/personalize-your-profile) says that a profile cover is available for a **Pinterest business account**. The image must be at least **800×450 pixels**, and Pinterest recommends a horizontal **16:9 aspect ratio**. Pinterest also says that anyone on Pinterest can view the profile, so treat the cover as public-facing content.

Those are the platform's published requirements and recommendation. They do not guarantee that every screen will show identical pixels. Keep the most important part of a logo, product photo or short message away from the outer edge so the cover remains clear after responsive cropping.

## Keep the important content in the safe area

Use the 16:9 canvas for the background and place the subject near the visual center. This is a practical composition rule, not an additional Pinterest measurement.

- Leave extra space around faces, logos and short text.
- Avoid putting essential details tight against the top, bottom or sides.
- Prefer a simple, high-contrast image that still reads at a narrow width.
- Preview the crop on both a wide screen and a phone-sized viewport.
- Do not place a full Pin layout inside the cover; the two surfaces have different proportions.

If the source is small, enlarging it will not restore detail. Start with a clean image at or above Pinterest's 800×450 minimum when possible. A larger 16:9 working file is fine, but it is a practical production choice rather than an official minimum.

## Resize a Pinterest cover locally in Chrome

1. **Choose a 16:9 canvas.** Set custom dimensions with at least 800×450 pixels.
2. **Open the image in FrameForge.** The extension prepares the image in the browser instead of sending it to a resizing service.
3. **Position the focal point.** Keep the face, mark or key visual away from the edges.
4. **Inspect the crop.** Shrink the preview and check that the subject is still recognizable.
5. **Export before uploading.** Save the finished image, then use Pinterest's own cover editor to make the final crop adjustment.

FrameForge is useful for a repeatable local crop when you want to prepare several brand or profile images. Verify the current extension listing and permissions before installing any browser tool.

## Do not confuse a cover with a Pinterest Pin

The [Pinterest Pin size guide](/blog/resize-image-for-pinterest-pin/) covers the vertical content format. Pinterest's [Pin performance guidance](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pin-performance-and-distribution) recommends a 2:3 ratio for image and video Pins between 15 and 60 seconds, while the profile cover guidance recommends 16:9. Use the canvas that matches the surface you are publishing to.

## Frequently asked questions

### What size should a Pinterest profile cover be?

For a Pinterest business profile, the image must be at least 800×450 pixels. Pinterest recommends a horizontal 16:9 aspect ratio. These are the published cover guidelines, not a promise that every device will show the same crop.

### Is a Pinterest profile cover the same as a Pin?

No. A cover belongs to a business profile. A Pin is separate content with its own formats, safe zones and metadata. Use a vertical Pin canvas for a Pin and a 16:9 canvas for the cover.
