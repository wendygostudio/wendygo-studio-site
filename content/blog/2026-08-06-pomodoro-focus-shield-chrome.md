A focus timer can tell you to work for 25 minutes, but a distracting tab can still be one click away. A Pomodoro Focus Shield for Chrome adds a small amount of friction between the session and the sites that usually interrupt it. The goal is not to lock down the whole browser. It is to protect the work block while keeping the tools, references and communication channels that the task genuinely needs.

## What a Focus Shield should do

The useful version is predictable. You choose a session, define the sites that tend to pull you away, and receive a warning or a block while the timer is active. When the session ends, the restriction should stop without forcing you to rebuild your browser setup.

| During a focus block | During the break |
| --- | --- |
| Reduce access to selected distractions | Review messages and feeds |
| Keep project tabs available | Open anything needed for the next block |
| Show the remaining session time | Decide what to focus on next |

> **Block the distraction, not the context.** If research, documentation or a team chat is part of the task, allow it and remove only the sites that repeatedly break your attention.

## Common Focus Shield mistakes

- Blocking every social or search site, including resources needed for the task.
- Changing the list every few minutes instead of starting the block.
- Treating a warning as a failure when it successfully helps you return.
- Making the block so long that a missed session feels impossible to recover from.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does a Focus Shield block the whole internet?
No. It is designed to reduce access to selected distracting sites during a session. Keep essential work context available.

### Can I use different sites for different projects?
Yes. A narrow list that matches the current task is less disruptive than one global list.

### Is Focus Shield parental-control software?
No. It is a focus aid and can be bypassed by disabling the extension.

### Does SlimeForge require an account?
No. The core timer, goals and companion are local-first and do not require an account.

### What duration should I start with?
Choose the shortest block that still lets you make visible progress, usually 15 or 25 minutes when building the habit.
