# Safe Margins for Social Media Images: A Chrome Workflow

Social platforms crop images in different places. A title that looks comfortable in a working file can sit too close to the edge in a feed, thumbnail or profile grid. Safe margins are the simple buffer that keeps the important part of an image readable after that crop.

You do not need a full design suite for this final preparation step. If the artwork already exists, FrameForge gives you a local Chrome workflow for checking the crop, keeping the subject in a safe area and exporting a platform-ready copy.

## What a safe margin protects

A safe margin is not a universal percentage. It depends on the platform preset, the image ratio and what must remain visible. Use a larger buffer when a platform overlays buttons or text, and a smaller one when the image is mainly a photograph with no edge-sensitive detail.

| Asset | Keep comfortably inside the frame |
| --- | --- |
| Headline or logo | Leave breathing room on every side |
| Face or product | Keep the focal point away from crop edges |
| Fine print | Move it inward or remove it |
| Decorative background | Let it reach the edge only when it can be cropped |

> A safe margin is a decision about information hierarchy: the content that must survive the crop belongs in the centre; supporting decoration can live outside it.

## A repeatable Chrome workflow

## How to check the result before publishing

View the export at the size people will actually see it. On a small preview, the focal point should be obvious and the headline should remain legible without touching the frame. Then open the image in a second tab and check the crop at 100%: a comfortable margin should look intentional, not like an accidental empty border.

For a set of platforms, repeat the check with each preset rather than stretching one export. A square crop, a landscape thumbnail and a vertical post have different safe areas. Keeping separate exports also avoids forcing a single compromise crop on every channel.

## FAQ

### Should every image use the same margin?

No. The correct margin follows the platform ratio and the content. Keep the important subject consistent, but allow decorative backgrounds to change with the crop.

### Do safe margins require uploading the image?

No. FrameForge processes the image locally in Chrome, so the preparation step does not require an account or an upload.

### Can I prepare several sizes at once?

Yes. FrameForge Pro includes batch workflows for exporting multiple variants; for one image, choose each destination preset and check the result before publishing.

### Is FrameForge a replacement for a design suite?

No. It is a focused local raster editor. Use a design suite for building the composition, and FrameForge for the final crop, margin check and export.
