Best Pomodoro Timer Chrome Extension in 2026 — With a Virtual Pet

SlimeForge is a Pomodoro timer Chrome extension that adds a virtual pet and gamification to keep you focused. Your slime hatches and grows with every session you complete.

July 15, 2026

Most Pomodoro timers fail you the same way: you set 25 minutes, the alarm rings, you close the tab, and by the next morning you've forgotten the extension exists. SlimeForge fixes this with a virtual slime pet that lives inside your browser and grows only when you focus.

What Is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique splits your work into intervals — typically 25 minutes of focused work followed by a short break. After four cycles, you take a longer break. It's one of the most researched time-management methods because it makes the act of sitting down to work feel manageable and bounded.

The problem isn't the technique. It's keeping a timer you actually open every day.

How SlimeForge Works as a Pomodoro Timer

SlimeForge runs entirely in your Chrome browser. No account, no server, nothing sent anywhere.

When you open the extension, you pick your session length:

  • 15 minutes — sprint mode for small tasks
  • 25 minutes — classic Pomodoro
  • 45 minutes — deep work blocks
  • 60 minutes — flow state sessions

Short and long breaks are included. SlimeForge tracks your streaks and session stats so you can see how consistent you've been over the week.

The Virtual Pet That Makes You Come Back

Here's the difference from a plain timer: your slime hatches after your first completed session.

Every focus session you complete earns you Brasas 🔥 — the in-extension currency. Your slime grows as you accumulate sessions. Ignore the extension for a few days and your pet doesn't grow. Open it and complete a session and it does. That feedback loop is surprisingly effective at turning "I should focus" into "I want to open SlimeForge."

There are 16 slime species to discover. You don't buy species — you find them by focusing.

Gamification That Goes Beyond a Streak Counter

SlimeForge adds several layers on top of the basic timer:

Missions give you specific goals to work toward (complete X sessions this week, reach a streak length, etc.). They're optional but help if you need short-term targets to stay motivated.

Crafting lets you combine items you earn from sessions to create new things for your slime. It adds a light idle-game element that you can engage with as much or as little as you want.

Friend visits — your slime can visit pages where other SlimeForge users are active. A small social layer that doesn't require a social account.

Companion mode — toggle this on and your slime walks across the pages you're browsing. Easy to turn off if you find it distracting.

Gemini Nano chat — your slime can have a short conversation with you via Gemini Nano, running fully on-device in 6 languages. No external API calls.

Ethical gacha: SlimeForge has a gacha system, but real money never buys random outcomes. If you pay, you get what you paid for. Randomness is earned through focus sessions, not purchases.

How to Get Started

  1. Install SlimeForge from the Chrome Web Store and pin the icon to your toolbar.
  2. Click the SlimeForge icon to open the extension panel.
  3. Choose your session length — 25 minutes is a good default start.
  4. Click Start. Work until the timer ends without closing the tab.
  5. Take your break when prompted, then start the next session.
  6. After your first completed session, your slime hatches. Keep going to help it grow.

The PRO trial is 5 days and unlocks the full feature set. The free version is functional as a timer and gives you the pet basics.

Who Is SlimeForge For?

SlimeForge works best for people who've tried plain Pomodoro timers and found them forgettable. If the problem is that you start a timer and then drift to Twitter because there's no consequence either way, a pet that only grows when you focus changes that calculus.

It's also a good fit for students and developers who spend long hours in front of a screen and want something that makes the focus-break cycle feel like progress rather than just a clock running.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SlimeForge free?
Yes. The free version includes the Pomodoro timer, streaks, and the virtual pet. A 5-day PRO trial unlocks the full feature set including all missions, crafting, and the extended companion features.

Does SlimeForge work offline?
Yes. The timer and pet run entirely locally. Gemini Nano chat is also on-device, so nothing requires an internet connection after installation.

Can I chat with my slime?
Yes, via Gemini Nano running on your device. The conversation is local and available in 6 languages (EN/ES/FR/DE/IT/PT).

What are Brasas?
Brasas 🔥 are the in-extension currency you earn by completing focus sessions. They're used for crafting and growing your slime — not purchasable with real money.

Does the real-money gacha exist?
SlimeForge has a gacha mechanic, but real money never buys random outcomes. Any purchase with real money gives you a known item. Randomness is reserved for Brasas earned through focus.

Will my slime die if I stop using the app?
No. Your slime doesn't have a health meter that depletes — it simply stops growing if you stop completing sessions. It will still be there when you come back.
