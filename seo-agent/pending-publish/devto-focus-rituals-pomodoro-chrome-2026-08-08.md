Starting is often harder than the work. A focus ritual reduces the choices between opening Chrome and doing the first useful action.

Try this four-step setup:

1. Write one observable outcome.
2. Choose a realistic 15 or 25 minute block.
3. Keep necessary research open and restrict only the sites that distract you.
4. Start the smallest action immediately.
