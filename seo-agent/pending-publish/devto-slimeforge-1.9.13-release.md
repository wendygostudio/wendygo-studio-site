SlimeForge 1.9.13 makes focus feel like visible progress.

Every completed Pomodoro helps a tiny animal-slime companion grow. You can choose 15, 25, 45 or 60 minutes, add a goal, and use Focus Shield to gently warn you about distracting sites or block them until the session ends.

The update also brings a larger world around the timer: daily missions, gentle streaks, minigames, a garden, a wardrobe, Pack Mode for up to three companions, and expeditions with materials and relics to discover.

Optional on-device AI can help turn a vague goal into a practical next step. The model runs locally when supported by the browser and device.

SlimeForge requires no account and includes no tracking. The free version includes the complete timer, basic Focus Shield and a weekly expedition.
